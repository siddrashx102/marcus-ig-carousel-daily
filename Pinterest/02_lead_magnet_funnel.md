# Lead Magnet & Funnel Architecture

## Why a lead magnet, not a direct-sell pin

Competitor research ([01_competitor_breakdown.md](01_competitor_breakdown.md)) shows the accounts skipping straight to "buy our eBook" links are almost all riding an existing off-Pinterest audience (IG/TikTok crossposts). Accounts building from Pinterest search traffic alone — the closest analog to this launch — funnel through a **free PDF first**, near-universally. Pinterest users are in browse/save/plan mode, not buy-now mode; asking for an email in exchange for a small free win converts far better than asking for money on the first touch.

---

## Recommended Freebie: "The 7-Day Stoic Reset Journal"

**Why this offer, specifically:**
- Matches the exact demand cluster seen repeatedly in research: "Stoic Journal Prompts," "Daily Journal of Stoic Reflection," "Stoic Journal for Self-Discipline" — all high-repeat search/pin patterns.
- 7 days is short enough to finish (high completion rate = high perceived value = more word-of-mouth/saves) but long enough to build a daily habit loop that primes the reader for the full "Unshakable" ebook, which is the natural "what's next" after a 7-day taste.
- Journal format lets each day double as its own pin (7 individual carousel/graphic pins from one asset), multiplying content output from one production pass.

**What's inside (keep production light — this is a lead magnet, not the product):**
- Day 1–7, one page each: a short Stoic principle (drawn from Marcus Aurelius / Epictetus / Seneca), one guided prompt, one "do this today" micro-action.
- Progression across the week: Day 1–2 = awareness (naming the overthinking/reactivity pattern), Day 3–5 = reframe (dichotomy of control, negative visualization), Day 6–7 = action (a discipline habit to carry forward) — this mirrors the funnel-stage logic already used for the Instagram content queue (value/relate → reframe → push), so the freebie and the paid content voice stay consistent.
- Last page: soft bridge to "Unshakable" — not a hard pitch, just "if this week helped, here's the next step" with the Gumroad link.

**Format:** single PDF, 9–10 pages, same dark/minimal aesthetic as the winning visual archetype (charcoal background, serif quote type, one Stoic-adjacent icon accent) — see [01_competitor_breakdown.md](01_competitor_breakdown.md) for the visual pattern this borrows from.

## Funnel Architecture

```
Pinterest pin (carousel or quote pin)
        │  "Get the free 7-Day Stoic Reset Journal"
        ▼
Landing page (single opt-in form, no fluff copy)
        │  email address → journal PDF delivered
        ▼
Email 1 (Day 0, immediate): deliver + set expectation
        ▼
Email 2 (Day 3): mid-journal check-in + reinforce identity ("you're already doing what most people won't")
        ▼
Email 3 (Day 7): journal's done → introduce "Unshakable" as the next step
        ▼
Gumroad checkout — thedigitalvault2025.gumroad.com/l/unshakable
```

**Placement note:** route the *majority* of pins to the journal opt-in, not the ebook directly — this matches the dominant competitor pattern. Reserve a small minority of pins (the D22+ full-push rows in the existing content calendar) for pins that link straight to the Gumroad page, aimed at warmer traffic (people re-pinning/following already) rather than cold search traffic.
