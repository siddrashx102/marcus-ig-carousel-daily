# 30-Day Pinterest Publishing Plan

## Cadence

Pinterest's own creator guidance and the volume seen from top accounts in research both point the same direction: consistency beats burst-posting. Target:

- **3 pins/day** minimum, ideally spread across the day rather than batch-posted (morning, midday, evening) — Pinterest's algorithm favors steady signal over time.
- Mix per day: **2 quote/reach pins + 1 funnel pin** (carousel or journal-mockup pin linking to the freebie or Unshakable). This mirrors Archetype 5 + Archetype 4 from the competitor breakdown — quote pins build board authority and saves, funnel pins convert.
- Every pin is a **fresh image** (new file, not a repost of a prior pin even to the same URL) — Pinterest's ranking explicitly favors newly-uploaded creative.

## Board Organization

Set up boards *before* day 1 so each pin has a clear home and contributes to topical authority:

1. **Stoic Quotes** — daily quote pins, broadest reach board
2. **Marcus Aurelius Daily Habits** — routine/habit-focused pins
3. **Stoic Journal & Printables** — the freebie + journal-format carousels (primary funnel board)
4. **Overcoming Overthinking** — pain-point-angle content, mental toughness
5. **Stoicism for Beginners** — entry-level educational carousels, broadest top-of-funnel SEO board

Every pin gets added to its most relevant board first, then optionally 1 secondary board (never more than 2 — over-boarding dilutes topical signal).

## 30-Day Calendar

| Days | Focus | Pin mix | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–5 | **Foundation** — pure reach, no hard funnel yet | 3 quote pins/day | Build initial board saves/follows before asking for anything; establishes topical signal for Pinterest's algorithm |
| 6–10 | **Freebie launch** | 2 quote pins + 1 journal-freebie pin/day | Introduce the 7-Day Stoic Reset Journal opt-in; test 2 different cover visuals (see A/B section) |
| 11–15 | **Educational carousels** | 2 quote pins + 1 educational carousel/day | "Dichotomy of control," "Stoic morning routine," "signs you need more discipline" — carousels build save-through and establish authority ahead of the sales push |
| 16–20 | **Freebie scale-up** | 2 quote pins + 1 freebie pin/day | Double down on whichever freebie cover/hook won the Day 6–10 A/B test |
| 21–25 | **Soft teaser** | 2 quote pins + 1 soft-teaser pin/day | Pins that mention "Unshakable" exists without a hard sell — matches the existing content calendar's D18–21 soft-teaser stage |
| 26–30 | **Full push** | 1 quote pin + 1 freebie pin + 1 direct Unshakable pin/day | Direct-to-Gumroad pins now justified — by this point there's an audience of repeat savers/followers, not just cold traffic |

## A/B Test Variations

Run these in parallel, not sequentially, so results aren't confounded by day-of-month effects:

1. **Cover hook framing:** Loss-frame ("Nothing kills you faster than your own mind"-style) vs. Direct Question ("Stuck in a mental maze?"-style) — same underlying content, two different Day 6 pin covers.
2. **Freebie CTA phrasing:** "Get your free PDF" vs. "Claim your free 7-day journal" — test on otherwise-identical carousel pins.
3. **Format:** Static single-image quote pin vs. multi-slide carousel for the same piece of content (e.g., a "5 Stoic habits" idea as both a single infographic and a 5-slide carousel).
4. **Visual palette:** Charcoal/black minimalist vs. warm off-white/cream minimalist — both seen in competitor research, worth confirming which performs better for this specific audience before locking the brand look.

Measure against saves and outbound clicks (not just impressions) — Pinterest surfaces high-save content more, so saves are the leading indicator to watch weekly, with email opt-ins as the actual funnel metric that matters.

## Review Checkpoint

At Day 15 and Day 30, pull Pinterest Analytics (top pins by saves + outbound clicks, top boards) and compare against which A/B variants won — adjust Days 16–30 (and the next month's plan) based on actual data rather than assumptions carried in from this research.
