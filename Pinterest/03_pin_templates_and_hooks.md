# Pin Templates, Hook Frameworks & SEO Metadata

## Top 5 Hook Frameworks (reverse-engineered from competitor research)

These are the formulas actually repeating across the highest-visibility pins found in research ([01_competitor_breakdown.md](01_competitor_breakdown.md)) — not generic copywriting theory.

**1. The Numbered Promise** — `[Number] [Things] to [Outcome]`
Seen everywhere: "10 Strategies To Manage Overthinking," "48 Stoic Exercises," "15 Stoic Tips For Mastering Yourself." A number sets a concrete expectation (this is finishable, scannable) which drives saves — Pinterest's core engagement signal.

**2. The Loss-Frame Pain Point** — `[Painful truth about inaction]`
"Nothing kills you faster than your own mind." No number, no list — just a stark statement that makes the pain point visceral before offering any solution. Works best as a scroll-stopper on quote-style pins.

**3. The Direct Question** — `Stuck in [specific bad state]? [Implied solution]`
"Stuck in a mental maze of a racing mind?" Names the reader's exact internal experience back to them — high relatability, primes the click for relief.

**4. The Authority Borrow** — `[Stoic figure]'s [Method/Rule/Way]`
"Seneca's Way," "The Marcus Aurelius Routine," "Rules For Life by Marcus Aurelius." Borrows 2,000 years of credibility instantly — no other self-improvement niche gets this for free.

**5. The Free-First Offer** — `Get [specific deliverable] as a free [format]`
"Get these pages as a free PDF download." The single most-repeated line in the entire dataset, appearing across unrelated accounts and countries. State the format (PDF, journal, checklist) explicitly — it sets a concrete expectation of what they're getting for their email.
