# Marcus carousel caption formula

Proven structure (ported from the @elev8ted.path-modeled formula used on the Mara account, `post_08082026/Carousel_Post.md`). Write fresh each run from that day's Hook/Topic, CTA, Pillar — this is a formula, not a copy source.

```
[Hook — contrarian reframe of the pain point, 1-2 lines]

[Thesis — the real cause, 1-2 lines]

---

Here are [N] things [that relate to the topic] (but aren't [wrong assumption]):

---

1. [point one]

2. [point two]

... one line per numbered slide ...

---

[Reframe tied to the Stoic quote used on the quote slide — Marcus Aurelius / Epictetus / Seneca, attributed]

---

[Payoff line — the version of them without this problem already exists / is close]

---

[Engagement question]

[Save-CTA line]

---

[SEO keyword block — 10-15 comma-separated phrases a reader might search, e.g. "stop overthinking, stoic mindset, discipline over motivation, marcus aurelius quotes"]

---

#Stoicism #Discipline #Mindset #SelfImprovement #MarcusAurelius [+ 2-3 more relevant tags]
```

**Funnel-stage CTA swap** (per Day number, see CLAUDE.md):
- D1-D17 (value): no product CTA, save/engagement only.
- D18-D21 (soft teaser): vague mention, no direct link.
- D22+ (full push): CTA line becomes the row's actual CTA column value, linking `https://thedigitalvault2025.gumroad.com/l/unshakable`.
